# Kickoff deck outline

Eleven slides, in this order, each filled from `KICKOFF-BRIEF.md`. Format-agnostic: the same outline
drives PowerPoint, Google Slides or Claude Design Slides. One idea per slide, bullets not paragraphs,
client-facing tone. Drop a slide only if its brief section is entirely `[TBD]`.

| # | Slide | Content (from brief section) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | Engagement name, Client, Vendor, date. Subtitle: "Project Kickoff". |
| 2 | **Objectives & Success** | The 2–4 objectives (§1) and the success criteria (§5). |
| 3 | **Scope** | In scope vs out of scope, side by side (§1). |
| 4 | **Deliverables & Milestones** | Milestones on a timeline; commercial model as a footnote (§1). |
| 5 | **Team & RACI** | Primary contacts (§1) and the RACI summary (§5). |
| 6 | **Ways of Working** | Methodology, sprint length, ceremonies, cadence, tooling, DoR/DoD (§3). |
| 7 | **Technical Approach** | Current/target stack, integrations, environments, hosting (§2). |
| 8 | **Non-Functional Requirements** | The NFR table — measurable targets only (§4). |
| 9 | **Governance & Communication** | Communication plan, reporting cadence, escalation, change control (§5). |
| 10 | **Risks, Assumptions & Dependencies** | Top risks, key assumptions, external dependencies (§6). |
| 11 | **Next Steps** | Open items and their owners (§ Open Items), and the immediate actions before work starts. |
